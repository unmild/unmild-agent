Unmild Agent — Privacy Policy

Your data stays on your device.

Unmild Agent is a local-first, bring-your-own-key (BYOK) desktop app. We do not run inference, we do not operate analytics, and we do not see your API keys, prompts, or project files. This policy explains verified behavior — what stays local, what leaves your machine only when you tell it to, and how the marketing site at https://unmild.store/agent/ handles visitors.

Effective: 8 May 2026 Last updated: 8 May 2026 Controller: Unmild Applies to: Unmild Agent app + unmild.store/agent website
TL;DR — Keys and projects are stored in %LOCALAPPDATA%\UnmildAgent\projects.json (and %LOCALAPPDATA%\UnmildAgent-dev\ in dev builds) on your Windows PC. History lives in history-{id}.sqlite (80 messages, FTS5 WAL) + work-{project}-{run}.jsonl. Network calls go only to the base_url you configure — verbatim, no /v1 rewrite — and only when you press Test Connection or Send.

1. Introduction

Unmild Agent (Agent) is a paid native Windows desktop application built with Rust + egui. It is an autonomous software-engineering agent — it lists, reads, writes and deletes files, runs shell commands, controls an embedded browser, and keeps durable project notes — always sandboxed to the working folder(s) you select.

This Privacy Policy describes how Unmild (we, us) handles information when you (a) use the Agent app and (b) visit the marketing site at https://unmild.store/agent/. We wrote it against verified app behavior, not marketing claims. If something here conflicts with what the app does, the app wins — please tell us so we can fix the text.

No Unmild account. No Unmild cloud. The Agent has no sign-in to Unmild, no telemetry endpoint, and no Unmild-hosted model. You bring your own API key and tell the Agent which API base URL to call.

3. What we process — and where it stays

By default nothing is sent to Unmild. The following stays on your device unless you explicitly trigger a network call (§4):

  • Credentials: base_url and api_key per profile (stored in %LOCALAPPDATA%\UnmildAgent\projects.json and in the per-run PersistedRef). Plaintext at rest — see §9.
  • Projects & working folders: paths you pick, per-project persisted state, and the 40 × 1,000-character durable remember notes (memory).
  • History: up to 80 messages per conversation in history-{id}.sqlite (FTS5, WAL mode) plus append-only work-{project}-{run}.jsonl and tool outputs (tool/terminal, tool/browser PNGs). Turn attachments are stored as base64.
  • Files & terminal output: file contents you ask the Agent to read/edit, and command output from run_command — confined by the sandbox (blocks .., absolute paths, symlinks, deep nesting). The app verifies writes and re-runs builds/tests before reporting success.
  • Context & UI state: selected model (context_limit_for — Claude 200k / gpt-4o 128k), effort level (max_tokens 1024–16384), permission mode (AskFirst / Auto-edit / Full access), and tool limits (MAX_TOOL_STEPS 1000, describe_tool listing 48-char, MAX_IMAGE_BYTES 5 MiB / MAX_PDF_BYTES 20 MiB / MAX_TEXT_BYTES 100 KiB).

Status messages redact content as [redacted]. Compaction (needs_compaction / compact + handoff) preserves your original request so a long task can resume without re-exposing data to us.

4. What leaves your device — and when

Network calls happen only in two user-initiated cases. The destination is always the base_url you entered — passed verbatim via normalize_base (no forced /v1 append). If the first attempt fails, we try one fallback candidate_bases = [base, base + "/v1"].

4.1 Test Connection — GET models

When you click Test Connection, the app does a GET to list models at your base URL to verify the key. No prompt or file content is sent in this call.

4.2 Chat completions — POST messages

When you press Send, the app POSTs to {base}/chat/completions (Wire::OpenAi, Authorization: Bearer <key>) or {base}/messages (Wire::Anthropic, x-api-key + anthropic-version: 2023-06-01) — single auth header per wire, no dual-header. Payload is your conversation (including file excerpts, tool outputs, and attachments you included, base64-encoded) and generation parameters (ANTHROPIC_MAX_TOKENS 4096, effort-level max_tokens). Streaming retries up to 2× on interrupted/closed, with MAX_SEND_RETRIES 12, MAX_RATE_WAIT 60s, MAX_TRANSIENT_WAIT 30s.

That provider — not Unmild — receives and processes the request under its own terms and privacy policy (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter, AgentRouter, or a self-hosted OpenAI-compatible endpoint). Unmild does not proxy, log, or see this traffic.

Your choice, your bill. Model costs are between you and the provider. The Agent never inserts a middle server to meter usage.

5. Bring your own key (BYOK)

You supply the API key. The Agent treats it as a secret you own: it is stored locally (see §3), used only as a Bearer / x-api-key header to your chosen base URL, and never sent to Unmild. You can rotate or revoke the key at the provider at any time; the Agent will simply fail closed until you update it.

Do not paste a key into a shared screen, issue, or prompt you intend to publish. Status/UI redaction hides values as [redacted], but raw history on disk retains what you sent — that is local history you control (see §8).

6. Website — no cookies, no analytics

The marketing site at https://unmild.store/agent/ is a static single page (index.html ~37 kB, assets/css/style.css ~24 kB, assets/js/app.js + 20 images). We set no cookies, run no analytics scripts (no GA, no Meta Pixel, no session replay), and embed no third-party trackers.

  • Fonts: Instrument Sans, JetBrains Mono, Newsreader are loaded from fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com (Google Fonts CDN). When your browser fetches them, Google receives your IP, User-Agent, and referrer per its own policy. If you block Google Fonts, the site falls back to system fonts — no breakage.
  • Store links: Get on Microsoft Store points to https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9P3RXL6LD5F1… — Microsoft handles that visit under the Microsoft Privacy Statement.
  • Logs: Like any web server, our host may produce basic access logs (IP, timestamp, path, UA) for security and delivery. We do not use them for profiling and we do not sell them. Where feasible we retain them ≤ 30 days then discard.

8. Retention & deletion

On your device: data persists until you delete it. Remove a project folder from the Agent’s project picker and delete %LOCALAPPDATA%\UnmildAgent\projects.json, history-{id}.sqlite, and work-{project}-{run}.jsonl to erase local history. In dev builds the path is %LOCALAPPDATA%\UnmildAgent-dev\. There is no server-side copy to purge — deletion is local file deletion.

With your AI provider: retention follows the provider’s policy for API calls (some retain for abuse monitoring for 30 days, some offer zero-retention). Review your provider’s DPA before sending sensitive code or data.

Website logs: see §6 — short-lived, ≤ 30 days where under our control.

9. Security

We built the Agent to minimize attack surface: Rust + egui native binary (windows_subsystem = "windows"), ureq + rustls for TLS (no system OpenSSL), and a working-folder sandbox that rejects .., absolute-path escapes, symlinks, and deeply nested writes. Tool outputs are bounded (MAX_IMAGE_BYTES 5 MiB etc.) to avoid resource exhaustion.

Important limitation — plaintext keys: API keys in projects.json / PersistedRef are stored plaintext on disk, protected only by Windows file ACLs and full-disk encryption (BitLocker) if you have it enabled. Anyone with access to your Windows profile can read them. Treat your device as the security boundary, lock it, use BitLocker, and rotate keys if you suspect exposure. We are evaluating OS credential-store integration for a future release.

No software is perfectly secure. Keep Windows and the Agent updated via the Store, use a dedicated API key with spend limits, and avoid pasting secrets you do not intend to send to the provider.

10. Legal bases (GDPR / UK GDPR)

Where GDPR applies, we process website-visit data (if any) on these bases:

  • Legitimate interests — to serve the static site securely, fix bugs, and prevent abuse (we balance this against your right to a tracker-free visit).
  • Contract — Store purchase/licensing via Microsoft where you buy the app.
  • Consent — only if you give it (e.g., you email us). You can withdraw consent by asking us to delete the thread.

The Agent app itself does not create a controller relationship with Unmild for your prompts — when you hit Send, you instruct a transfer to the provider you chose (see §13). We are not a joint controller of that traffic.

11. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (EEA, UK, California, etc.) you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object, port, and not be subject to automated decision-making. Because the Agent keeps data locally, you can exercise most of these rights yourself instantly by deleting local files (§8).

For any data we do hold (e.g., an email you sent us, or Store aggregate data that could identify you), write via the contact in §15 with subject [Privacy]. We respond within 30 days and verify identity proportionate to risk — we will not ask for more data than needed. You can also lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority (e.g., your EU DPA or the UK ICO). California residents: we do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA.

12. Children

Unmild Agent is not directed at children and is not intended for use by children under 13 (and under 16 where GDPR requires a higher threshold). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data via the website contact, tell us and we will delete it promptly.

13. International transfers

There is no transfer to Unmild — your API key and prompts never touch our servers to transfer in the first place. When you press Send, the Agent opens a direct TLS connection from your PC to the base_url you configured. That may be in another country (e.g., US-hosted providers). Safeguards for that leg are between you and the provider; review their DPA / Standard Contractual Clauses before use. The static website is served globally via standard hosting/CDN — same short-lived access logs as §6.
